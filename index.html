ABOUT

About Me

My research uses carbonate geochemistry to understand the thermal, hydrological, and environmental histories preserved in sedimentary rocks. I am particularly interested in what siderite clumped isotope temperatures record and how those signals are modified during burial and diagenesis.

RESEARCH

Research Interests

01

Siderite Clumped Isotopes

Understanding the temperature information recorded by siderite Δ47 thermometry and evaluating its potential as a terrestrial paleoclimate proxy.

02

Diagenesis & Water–Rock Interaction

Investigating carbonate recrystallization, dissolution– reprecipitation, fluid alteration, and the preservation of primary geochemical signals.

03

Paleoclimate

Reconstructing continental temperatures and hydrologic conditions from Late Cretaceous–Paleogene sedimentary basin records.
