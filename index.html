ABOUT

About Me

I'm a Ph.D. candidate in the Department of Geosciences at Penn State, working with Dr. Miquela Ingalls. My dissertation uses bulk and clumped isotopes (Δ47) of siderite (ferrous iron carbonate) to reconstruct Earth's past surface temperature and hydroclimate, and to understand how post-depositional diagenesis during burial can alter primary geochemical signatures. Before Penn State, I received my B.S. in Geology from the China University of Geosciences (Beijing). As an undergraduate, I studied Mg isotope alteration during the aragonite–calcite transition, and carbon isotope changes during and after the Marinoan Snowball Earth glaciation.

Starting in January 2027, I will be joining Dr. Daniel Ibarra's group at Brown University as a postdoctoral scholar. At Brown, I will be working on triple oxygen isotopes in a range of materials (such as carbonates and silicates), as well as carbonate clumped isotopes, contributing to related projects.

RESEARCH

Research Projects

01

Siderite Isotopes During Burial Diagenesis

Comparing basins with contrasting burial histories to separate the diagenetic processes that alter siderite oxygen and clumped isotopes (Δ47): water–rock alteration in the shallowly buried Paleocene strata of the Wind River Basin, and solid-state reordering in the deeply and long-buried Pennsylvanian strata of the Appalachian Basin.

02

Paleocene Terrestrial Climate in the Wind River Basin

Reconstructing Paleocene continental temperatures and hydroclimate from siderite clumped isotopes, combining sedimentology, petrography, and geochemistry.

He et al., 2026, Journal of Sedimentary Research →

03

Carbon Isotopes in the Marinoan Snowball Earth

Using cap carbonate carbon isotopes to constrain seawater δ13C and the marine carbon cycle during the deglaciation of the Marinoan Snowball Earth.

He et al., 2021, Global and Planetary Change →

04

Mg Isotopes in the Aragonite–Calcite Transition

Measuring Mg isotopes in the Pleistocene Key Largo Limestone to examine Mg isotope behavior during the early diagenetic transition from aragonite to calcite.

He, Ning et al., 2020, Chemical Geology →
